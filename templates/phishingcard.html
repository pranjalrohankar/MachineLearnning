Phishing Attacks Explained

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers trick individuals into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords or credit card numbers, by pretending to be a trustworthy entity.

Deceptive Communication

Attackers send fake emails, messages, or websites that appear legitimate to trick victims.

Information Theft

Victims unknowingly provide sensitive information, such as login credentials or financial details.

Security Breach

Attackers use the stolen information for malicious purposes, such as identity theft or financial fraud.
