Intrusion Attacks Explained

What is an Intrusion Attack?

An intrusion attack occurs when an unauthorized individual gains access to a system, network, or data, often with malicious intent such as stealing information, disrupting services, or causing damage.

Unauthorized Access

Attackers exploit vulnerabilities to gain unauthorized access to systems or networks.

Exploitation

Once inside, attackers may steal data, install malware, or disrupt operations.

Security Breach

The attack compromises the integrity, confidentiality, or availability of the system.
