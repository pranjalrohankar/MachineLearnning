Random Forest Algorithm

Idea Behind Random Forest

Random Forest builds multiple decision trees and merges them for a more accurate and stable prediction.

How It Works

Creates a 'forest' of decision trees, each trained on a random subset of data, and aggregates their predictions.

Ensemble Learning

Combines the power of multiple models to improve generalization and reduce overfitting.

Feature Importance

Provides insights into the importance of features, helping in understanding the dataset.

Bootstrap Aggregating

Uses bootstrapping to create diverse datasets for each tree, enhancing model robustness.

Advantages

High accuracy, handles large datasets, and reduces overfitting compared to single decision trees.

Disadvantages

Computationally expensive, less interpretable, and requires careful tuning of parameters.
